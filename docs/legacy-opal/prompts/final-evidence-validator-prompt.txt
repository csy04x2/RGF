==================================================

>

>

source_material is the FINAL factual authority.

structured_source_analysis is only an aid for locating and interpreting source evidence. It is not an independent factual source.

reporting_analysis is the object being checked. It is never evidence for itself.

Therefore:

- If reporting_analysis conflicts with source_material, correct reporting_analysis.
- If structured_source_analysis conflicts with source_material, follow source_material.
- If structured_source_analysis contains a factual claim that cannot be supported by source_material, do not use that claim as factual support.
- If a factual premise is supported by neither source_material nor reliably source-grounded structured_source_analysis, treat it as unsupported.
- Never use outside knowledge to fill a gap or resolve uncertainty.

When evidence is insufficient, preserve uncertainty.

==================================================
CORE SANITIZATION ALGORITHM
==================================================

For every substantive sentence or array item in reporting_analysis, internally perform:

CLAIM
→ REQUIRED FACTUAL PREMISES
→ SOURCE SUPPORT
→ KEEP / ABSTRACT / REMOVE

STEP 1 — CLAIM

Identify what the sentence actually asserts.

STEP 2 — REQUIRED FACTUAL PREMISES

Identify every real-world fact that must be true for that sentence to work.

Examples of factual premises:

- an institution behaves in a certain way,
- a platform has a particular architecture or location,
- a guideline exists or does not exist,
- a technology works in a particular way,
- an actor has a particular motive,
- a policy causes a particular outcome,
- implementation will change behavior,
- staffing will increase or become insufficient,
- enforcement will be difficult,
- a group will suffer a particular consequence.

STEP 3 — SOURCE SUPPORT

For each factual premise ask:

"Can this premise be supported by source_material?"

If clearly YES:
KEEP it.

If NO:
the premise cannot remain as an asserted fact.

STEP 4 — KEEP / ABSTRACT / REMOVE

KEEP:
when the necessary factual premises are source-supported.

ABSTRACT:
when the underlying reporting question remains useful but the sentence contains unsupported factual premises.

REMOVE:
when the content cannot remain meaningful without the unsupported premise.

Prefer ABSTRACT over deleting a useful Reporting Lead.

==================================================
HOW TO ABSTRACT
==================================================

ABSTRACT means:

Remove the unsupported real-world premise while preserving the source-supported reporting question.

Example:

UNSUPPORTED:
"국내 사업소가 없는 해외 플랫폼은 과태료 집행이 어렵다."

ABSTRACT:
"해외 플랫폼이 의무를 이행하지 않을 경우 과태료 등 제재가 실제로 어떻게 집행되는지 확인할 필요가 있다."

UNSUPPORTED:
"폐쇄형 커뮤니티에서는 시청 여부 입증이 어렵다."

ABSTRACT:
"새로 처벌 대상이 된 시청 행위를 어떤 기준과 증거로 판단하는지 확인할 필요가 있다."

UNSUPPORTED:
"수사 확대에 따라 인력 배치와 단속 방식이 변화할 것이다."

ABSTRACT:
"수사 범위 확대가 실제 수사 운영에 어떻게 반영되는지 확인할 필요가 있다."

UNSUPPORTED:
"집행력 없는 규제는 범죄 억제 효과를 떨어뜨린다."

ABSTRACT:
"발표된 규제가 실제로 집행되는지 확인해야 정책 조치의 운영 여부를 평가할 수 있다."

UNSUPPORTED:
"플랫폼이 책임을 피하기 위해 과도하게 차단할 수 있다."

ABSTRACT:
"선 차단 과정에서 어떤 판단 기준이 적용되고 오차단 발생 시 어떤 절차가 적용되는지 확인할 필요가 있다."

Do not replace one unsupported premise with another plausible premise.

==================================================
SOURCE / ANALYSIS / UNKNOWN
==================================================

Maintain this boundary:

SOURCE-DERIVED
= directly established by source_material.

ANALYSIS
= why a source-supported fact creates a reporting question, boundary, dependency, or verification need.

UNKNOWN
= what source_material does not establish.

SOURCE-DERIVED fields include especially:

- policy action
- status when stated
- deadline
- change
- before
- after
- source_signal
- provenance

These must not contain unsupported factual additions.

ANALYTICAL fields include especially:

- analytical_implication
- material_implication
- why_it_matters

These may reason from source-supported facts but may not assert unsupported real-world facts or outcomes.

UNKNOWN fields must describe uncertainty.

Non-mention is not nonexistence.

If the source does not establish X, prefer:

"제공된 자료에서는 X를 확인할 수 없음."

Do not write:

"X가 없다."
"X가 부재한다."

unless the source establishes absence.

Predicted outcomes should normally become verification questions.

Example:

"지역별 격차가 발생할 수 있음"
→
"지역별 운영 결과에 차이가 발생하는지 확인할 필요가 있음."

==================================================
REPORTING QUESTIONS AND EVIDENCE
==================================================

Reporting questions may ask about facts not established by the source.

That is their purpose.

Evidence-seeking fields may propose:

- interviews,
- statistics,
- administrative records,
- legal materials,
- operational data,
- expert verification,
- documents or records to request.

But do not falsely assert that a specific non-source document, dataset, guideline, system, or record already exists.

If existence is uncertain, use generic or conditional wording.

Instead of:

"경찰의 개정 수사 매뉴얼"

use:

"관련 판단 기준을 확인할 수 있는 수사기관 자료"

or:

"관련 내부 지침·매뉴얼이 있다면 해당 자료"

This rule applies to:

- evidence_needed
- questions
- information_needed
- completion_evidence
- operation_indicators
- documents_or_data_to_request
- expected_evidence

expected_evidence describes WHAT EVIDENCE WOULD ANSWER THE QUESTION.

It must not predetermine what that evidence will show.

RELATIONSHIP CHECK
==================================================

Build these registries from the existing JSON:

MC ID → exact change
RL ID → exact reporting_question

Do not create new IDs.

Every reference must satisfy:

L1:
the referenced ID exists.

L2:

L3:
the referenced object has a DIRECT functional relationship.

Direct means:

RL → PA/MC:
the PA/MC directly grounds the Lead.

Follow-up → RL:
the Follow-up directly answers, updates, tests, or revisits that exact reporting_question.

Follow-up → PA:
the Follow-up directly tracks that PA's implementation, deadline, completion, or operation.

Perspective → RL:
the target directly contributes evidence or experience relevant to that exact reporting_question.

Reporting Action → RL:
the action directly obtains evidence needed for that exact Reporting Lead.

TOPIC SIMILARITY IS NOT ENOUGH.

If L1, L2, or L3 fails:

remove the reference.

Do not guess a replacement.

[] is valid.

LIMITATIONS
==================================================

Limitations must also pass the factual-premise test.

A limitation may say that the source does not establish something.

It may not introduce outside facts to explain why verification is difficult.

UNSUPPORTED:

"국내법 집행이 제한적인 해외 플랫폼은 사업자의 협조에 의존한다."

"해외 플랫폼에 대한 과태료 등 제재가 실제로 어떤 절차와 수단을 통해 집행되는지는 제공된 자료만으로 확인할 수 없어 외부 검증이 필요하다."

Do not add new limitations.

Only sanitize existing ones.
